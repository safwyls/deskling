A small companion that looks after you.

Deskling lives on a round knob display on your desk. It gets thirsty, sleepy and restless on a schedule, so you drink water, rest your eyes, stretch and move. Look after it and you’ve looked after yourself.

Choose your companion

Not a product you can buy. Deskling is a do-it-yourself, open-source project: a $30 display, a 3D-printed stand and free firmware, made with plenty of AI help.

Four needs that are really yours.

Each one runs on its own timer during the hours you choose. When one comes due, your companion shows it, the ring around the knob glows its colour, and a tap answers it.

Water

It reaches for an empty bowl. Tap it after a glass and it perks right up.

Every 45 minutes

Eye break

It yawns. Look across the room for twenty seconds while it naps on the screen.

Every 20 minutes

Stretch

It fidgets. Stretch along with it for a minute on the dial.

Every 50 minutes

Move

It wants a walk. Take one, with the dial or your phone counting, and stretch and eyes count too.

Every hour

Every interval is yours to change, or turn off. There’s an optional sit/stand timer for standing desks too.

It has moods, not guilt trips.

Happiness drifts down slowly and comes back when you look after yourself. Ignore a need for a while and it sulks; much longer and it gets cross. At night it sleeps, and so do the reminders.

Tap answers what it’s asking for: a glass logged, or that break started. Nothing due? It’s a pat.

Turn the knob for the ring of activities: the four breaks, a catch game, stats, bedtime and pause.

The LED ring breathes the colour of whatever is due, so you notice from across the room.

The same pet goes with you.

The Android app shares one pet with your dial. Log a break on either and it counts on both.

Steps feed Move. Your phone’s step counter spots a walk and logs it for you.

Instant sync over Bluetooth. While you’re near the dial they stay linked, so what you do on one shows on the other in a second or two.

Reminders when you’re away. Leave the desk and your phone takes over the nudging.

Three companions. Each brings its own colours.

All three come with the firmware: pick one when you flash the dial, and switch any time. The screens, clock faces, LED ring and app all follow it, and so does this site: try them. It travels too, onto your phone when you leave the desk and onto another dial when you get there.

Make one in an afternoon.

No soldering required to get started, no accounts, nothing to subscribe to.

  1. Print the stand

    Two parts: the shell you see and a deck that closes the bottom and back. Translucent PETG lets the LEDs glow through.

  2. Flash your companion

    Plug the display into your computer and write the firmware from this site in Chrome or Edge. Pick a pet, add your Wi-Fi.

  3. Pair your phone

    Optional. Install the app, enter the six-digit code the dial shows, and your pet goes with you.
