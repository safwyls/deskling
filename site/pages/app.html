Your pet, in your pocket.

Deskling for Android is where your companion goes when you leave the desk. Bring it along from the dial, and it counts your steps toward Move and reminds you itself; leave it at a dial again, yours or another, or give it to a friend’s phone.

Download for Android

Android 8 or newer

It isn’t on the Play Store. You install it straight from this page, which Android calls sideloading. The steps are below, and the source is in the repository if you’d rather build it yourself.

Install it

  1. Download it on your phone. Open this page on the phone and tap Download for Android. If it’s on your computer, send the file to the phone any way you like: a cable, a cloud drive, a message to yourself.
  2. Open the file. Tap the finished download in the notification shade, or find deskling.apk in the Files app under Downloads.
  3. Let your browser install apps. The first time, Android says your phone isn’t allowed to install unknown apps from this source. Tap Settings, switch on Allow from this source, then go back. The setting is per app, so it only applies to the browser or file manager you opened it from, and you can switch it off again afterwards.
  4. Install. Tap Install. Google Play Protect may warn that it doesn’t recognise the developer; that’s because the app isn’t from the Play Store. If you’re happy to go ahead, choose More details and then Install anyway.
  5. Open it and say yes to what it asks. Then pair it with your dial; The phone app in the build guide walks through it.

What it asks for, and why

  • Nearby devices (Bluetooth): to find your dial and stay linked to it. On Android 11 and older this shows up as Location, which is how those versions grant Bluetooth scanning; the app doesn’t use your location.
  • Physical activity: to count steps toward Move.
  • Notifications: for reminders while your companion is with you, and the quiet one that shows while it’s linked to a dial.

It talks only to your dial, over Bluetooth or your own Wi-Fi. There are no accounts and nothing goes to a server.

Updating

Download the new version from this page and install it the same way. It installs over the old one, and your pet, your pairing and your settings stay.

Brazil, Indonesia, Singapore or Thailand

Since September 30, 2026, phones in these countries only install apps from developers registered with Google. Deskling isn’t registered, so there you’ll need Android’s advanced flow: in Developer options, switch on Allow apps from unverified developers. It asks you to wait 24 hours once, then installs work as above. Or install from a computer with adb install deskling.apk.

Google plans the same rules for everywhere else in 2027.
