Put Deskling on your display.

Pick a companion, set it up the way you like, and write Deskling to the CrowPanel straight from this page over USB. You’ll need Chrome or Edge on a computer and a USB cable that carries data.

Wi-Fi

2.4 GHz networks only (the ESP32-S3 has no 5 GHz radio). The password goes from this page to your display and nowhere else. Wi-Fi is optional: the dial works without it, and the phone app can sync over Bluetooth.

Connect and write

Plug the display into this computer, press the button below and pick the dial from the list your browser shows (often “USB JTAG/serial debug unit” or a COM port). Keep the cable in until it restarts.


          
If it won’t connect: hold the BOOT button on the back of the display, tap RESET, let go of BOOT, then try again. More help.
