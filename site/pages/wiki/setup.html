First run and Wi-Fi

Your companion appears as soon as the firmware starts. Wi-Fi is how it learns the time, which decides when it’s awake and when the reminders run.

Getting around

On the pet screenDoes
Tap the screenAnswers what your pet is asking for (logs a glass, or starts that break). Nothing due? It’s a pat.
Turn the knobOpens the ring of activities: Water, Eye break, Stretch, Move, Sit/Stand (if on), Play, Stats, Bedtime, Pause and Back.
Press the knobOpens the menu: Activities, Settings, Back.

Every menu ends with a Back item, so you never need a long press.

Wi-Fi

If you entered your network on the firmware page, the dial joins it on first start and sets its clock from the internet. Otherwise, there are three ways:

  • From the phone app: Settings, then the dial’s Wi-Fi. The app asks the dial which networks it can see; pick one and type the password on your phone.
  • From any phone’s browser: on the dial, Settings > Set up from phone. It starts its own little network and shows a QR code to join it. The setup page usually opens by itself; if it doesn’t, the second QR code opens it. Pick your network there.
  • On the knob: Settings > Wi-Fi lists networks; typing a password with a knob is slow, so the other two are nicer.

The dial only does 2.4 GHz Wi-Fi. If your router combines 2.4 and 5 GHz under one name, that’s fine; a 5 GHz-only network won’t show up.

Hours and time zone

Settings > Reminders sets each reminder’s interval, the hours your pet is awake (8 am to 8 pm unless you chose otherwise) and the time zone. Outside those hours it sleeps, the timers stop, and nothing nags you.

Worth a look

  • Home screen: clock style (six to choose from), date, status icons and the “next reminder” line.
  • LED alignment: nudges the LED pattern so the lit point on the ring lines up with the screen. The ring moves as you turn, so you can see it.
  • Nap timer: after this long without a touch, your pet dozes and the screen dims.
  • Poses: plays every mood and activity, handy for seeing your companion’s whole range.
  • Last restart: why the dial last restarted. After a crash it shows a QR code with the details, which helps a lot when reporting a bug.
