Flash the firmware

The quickest way is the firmware page on this site: pick a companion, fill in your settings, plug in the display and press one button. This page explains what that button does, and what to try when it doesn’t.

From the browser

  1. Open the firmware page in Chrome or Edge on a computer. (Web Serial, which lets a page talk to a USB device, isn’t in Firefox, Safari or phone browsers.)
  2. Choose Wisp, Crumb or Sprig. All three are in the one firmware; a fresh install starts the dial with the one you pick, and you can switch later on the dial (Settings > Companion) or in the app.
  3. Choose Fresh install for a new display, or Update to keep everything already on it.
  4. For a fresh install, fill in your settings. Wi-Fi is optional.
  5. Plug the display in with a USB-C data cable, press Write to the display and pick it from the list your browser shows.
  6. Wait for the progress bar. The dial restarts with your companion on it.

What gets written

The display has 16 MB of flash, laid out like this. A fresh install writes all four pieces plus your settings; an update writes only the app.

AddressWhatFresh installUpdate
0x0BootloaderYes—
0x8000Partition tableYes—
0x9000Settings (NVS, 20 KB)Yours, built in the pageKept
0xE000Boot dataYes—
0x10000The app: firmware and animations (up to 14 MB)YesYes

Your settings never leave the page: it builds the settings partition in your browser, in the same format as Espressif’s nvs_partition_gen tool (we check them against each other byte for byte), and writes it straight to the display. That includes your Wi-Fi password.

If it won’t connect

What you seeTry
The display isn’t in the browser’s listA different cable (many only charge), a different USB port, or unplug and replug. On Linux, add yourself to the dialout group and log in again.
“The display didn’t answer”Put it in download mode: hold BOOT, tap RESET, let go of BOOT. Then press Write again.
It stops partwayUsually a loose cable or a hub. Plug straight into the computer and write again; nothing is harmed by a partial write.
It finishes but the screen stays darkPress RESET. If the dial still won’t start, do a Fresh install.

From the command line

With Espressif’s esptool and images from your own build (PlatformIO writes firmware.factory.bin for a full install and firmware.bin for the app):

esptool.py --chip esp32s3 write_flash 0x0 firmware.factory.bin   # fresh install: erases your settings
esptool.py --chip esp32s3 write_flash 0x10000 firmware.bin       # update: keeps them

From source

The firmware is a PlatformIO project in firmware/, one build with every companion in it:

cd firmware
pio run -t upload
