Get the display ready

Out of the box the CrowPanel runs Elecrow’s demo. Before flashing, it helps to know where its ports and buttons are and how you’ll power it on your desk.

What’s in the box

  • The display: a round touchscreen inside a knob that turns and clicks, on an ESP32-S3 board.
  • A 50 cm cable from the board’s small 4-pin MX1.25 power connector to USB.
  • A 4-pin Dupont cable for the expansion header (not needed for Deskling).

Ports and buttons

ThingWhat Deskling uses it for
USB-CFlashing from the browser, and the boot log if something goes wrong.
MX1.25 power connector5 V power, with the cable in the box.
BOOT and RESETDownload mode, if the browser can’t connect: hold BOOT, tap RESET, let go of BOOT.
Knob (turn and press) and touchEverything on the dial. A tap on the screen works like a press almost everywhere.
LED ringBreathes the colour of whatever is due.

Power on your desk

Any 5 V USB supply works, through the USB-C port or the MX1.25 cable in the box. Deskling is happy on a laptop port or a phone charger.

For a tidier stand, solder a pair of wires to the board’s 5 V input and ground on its underside. They run down to the USB-C socket in the back of the stand, either straight or through the battery board, so the dial runs from a single cable or none at all (Print the stand has the wiring). That’s optional, and if you’re not comfortable soldering, the supplied cable does the same job.

Whatever you do to the power, keep the USB-C data port reachable. Firmware updates go over it.
