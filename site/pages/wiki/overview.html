What you need

Deskling is three things: a $30 round display, a printed stand that holds it, and firmware you write to it from your browser. The phone app is optional.

Tools

  • A 3D printer, or a print service. All three parts fit on any bed; check your slicer’s preview for supports.
  • A computer with Chrome or Edge, which can talk to USB devices from a web page (Web Serial). Firefox and Safari can’t.
  • Optional: a soldering iron, if you want the stand’s own USB-C socket or the battery (see Print the stand).

The plan

  1. Print the stand while you wait for the display.
  2. Flash the firmware: pick your companion and your settings on the firmware page.
  3. First run: Wi-Fi gives it the time; reminders run during the hours you choose.
  4. Pair the app if you want your pet on your phone too.

This is a hobby project, not a kit. The steps here are what worked for us on our hardware; if something doesn’t match yours, the source is there to adapt.
