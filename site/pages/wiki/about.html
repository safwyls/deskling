Deep dive

Open source and AI help

Deskling looks like a product on this site, but it’s one person’s do-it-yourself project, built in the open with a lot of help from AI tools. Here’s exactly what that means.

Not for sale

There’s nothing to buy from us. You buy the display from Elecrow, print the ring yourself, and flash free firmware. No accounts, no cloud service, no subscription: the dial and the app talk only to each other and, for the clock, to public time servers.

The source

Firmware, phone app, this site, the ring’s model and the animation tools are all in one repository: github.com/safwyls/deskling.

What that means for you

  • It’s tested on one person’s hardware. The core of it has run on a real CrowPanel for days. The newest parts (instant Bluetooth sync, sit/stand, Crumb and Sprig’s colour schemes on the dial’s menus) were built and checked in software first and still need time on the device.
  • Read before you trust. AI-written code can be confidently wrong. The repository’s READMEs explain the design, and the tests cover the care game and the app’s screens.
  • The art is generated. The characters were drawn from original concept images by an image model, then animated by a video model. They aren’t the work of a hired illustrator.
