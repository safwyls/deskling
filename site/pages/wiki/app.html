The phone app

Deskling for Android shares one pet with your dial. It counts your steps toward Move, reminds you when you’re away from the desk, and stays in sync with the dial over Bluetooth while you’re near it.

Install

The app isn’t in the Play Store. Build it from app/ in the repository with Android Studio, or ./gradlew :app:assembleDebug, and install the APK on your phone. It needs Android 8 or newer.

Pair it with your dial

  1. On the dial: Settings > Phone app > Pair a phone. It shows a 6-digit code.
  2. In the app: Settings > Pair a dial. Enter the code.
  3. Pick your dial from the Bluetooth list or the Wi-Fi list (either works; the code is needed both ways), then Pair.

A dial remembers up to four phones. Settings > Phone app on the dial can forget them all.

What it does

  • One pet, two places. When the phone can reach the dial, the dial’s state is the truth: the phone sends what you logged (with the time you did it) and takes the dial’s state back. Away from the dial, the phone runs its own copy of the care game and catches up later.
  • Instant sync. While you’re nearby, the app keeps a Bluetooth link open. What you do on either side reaches the other within a second or two, and the app’s header says “in sync”. Android shows a quiet notification while it’s on; Settings > Stay connected to the dial turns it off.
  • Steps feed Move. Walk 250 steps within ten minutes and the app logs a Move for you. Both numbers are in Settings.
  • Reminders when you’re away. If the phone hasn’t been near the dial for ten minutes, it posts the reminders itself, each with a Done button.
  • The dial’s settings from your phone, including its Wi-Fi.
  • Your companion’s colours. The app follows whichever pet your dial runs. Without a dial, pick one in Settings.
