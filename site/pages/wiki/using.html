Living with Deskling

The rules of the game, for when you want to know why your pet looks the way it does.

Needs

NeedDefaultDone by
● WaterEvery 45 minA tap when it’s due, or Water in the ring: logs a glass.
● Eye breakEvery 20 minA 20-second rest: your pet naps while you look into the distance (the 20-20-20 rule).
● StretchEvery 50 minA one-minute stretch alongside your pet. Half a minute counts.
● MoveEvery hourA walk of at least two minutes on the dial’s timer, or steps counted by the app. A Move also checks off Stretch and the eye break.
● Sit/standOff; 45 / 15 minOptional, for standing desks. Tell it when you switch. It never affects the mood.

Timers only run during active hours, and not while paused or at bedtime. A new day starts them fresh.

Moods

Your pet’s mood follows its needs: content when nothing is due, happy when it’s very happy, thirsty, tired or restless when that need is due, sad when something has been ignored a while, mad when it’s been ignored a long time, and asleep at night, at bedtime and while napping. Each mood has its own animation and thought bubble.

Happiness, levels and streaks

  • Happiness drifts down about 10 points an hour, and 15 more an hour while something has been ignored for ten minutes or longer.
  • A break done on time (or at least halfway through its interval) gives the most back. A late one still counts. A very early one gives a little, so it can’t be farmed.
  • Breaks and pats give experience; levels take a little longer each time.
  • Four or more breaks in a day keeps your streak going.

The knob and the light

  • Tap the bare home screen: answers what your pet wants, or a pat (once a minute).
  • Turn: the ring of activities. The highlighted one is big in the middle with its timer; press to do it.
  • Play: catch the light as it runs around the rim. Five rounds, and it gets faster.
  • Bedtime sends your pet to sleep and stops the reminders until morning. While it’s asleep, the same spot on the ring says Wake up: that works outside its active hours too, and keeps it up (reminders and all) until the hours begin or you choose Bedtime. Pause stops reminders for an hour.
  • The LED ring breathes the colour of what’s due, quicker once it’s overdue, red when your pet is mad, and your companion’s own colour when all is well.
