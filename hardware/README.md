# Hardware

The desk stand for the CrowPanel 1.46" rotary display: three printed parts, all generated by one parametric
CadQuery script, with room inside for a battery.

| File | Part |
| --- | --- |
| `crowpanel_stand_v4.py` | The source. Every dimension is a named parameter at the top; run it to rebuild the parts and print a set of fit and clash checks. Needs `pip install cadquery`. |
| `crowpanel_v4_shell.step` / `.stl` | The shell (65 × 91 × 53 mm). The display loads from below through a tilted bore and stops on a printed shoulder; a collar hides everything below its black grip band. USB-C port in the back wall, a hole in the right side for the battery board's button. Translucent PETG carries the display's LED skirt out as a glow. |
| `crowpanel_v4_retainer.step` / `.stl` | The retainer ring, laid out to print (flange down; its front is trimmed flat to sit on the base, so it needs up to about 5 mm of support from the bed). Pushes the display's bottom plate up against the stop; 2 × M3×8 socket head screws through its ear into the shell. Unchanged from v3. |
| `crowpanel_v4_retainer_assembled.step` | The same retainer, in its place in the stand. |
| `crowpanel_v4_base.step` / `.stl` | The base plate: pins for the USB-C breakout, a pocket for the IP5306 board, wire clips, the 18350 cradle. 4 × M3×8 countersunk into heat-set inserts in the shell. |
| `v3/` | The previous version, with an inline USB-C pigtail and no charge board. |

The STEP files share one frame (desk at z = 0, the front toward -Y) and line up when imported together. The shell
STL is moved down 3 mm onto the bed, and the retainer files are in print orientation.

Electronics (v4): a 22 × 13 mm USB-C breakout (VBUS/GND/CC1/D-/D+/CC2, 5.1 kΩ CC pull-downs fitted) at the rear port,
an IP5306 charge + 5 V boost board (HW-775, 25 × 16 mm) on the right, an 18350 cell with 18650-holder contact plates
on the left. Wiring: breakout VBUS/GND → IP5306 VIN/GND; IP5306 BAT/GND → cell +/−; IP5306 OUT-5V/GND → the display's
5 V input. D+/D− stay unconnected; firmware goes over the display's own USB-C port. Without a battery, wire the
breakout straight to the display and leave out the IP5306.

Before printing the shell, measure your display: `STOP_A` (7.0 mm) is the height of its fixed base above the
bottom plate, taken from Elecrow's 3D model, which has been wrong around the skirt before. The board sizes come from
seller listings (±1–2 mm): check the breakout's hole spacing (`BRK_HOLE_X`) and the IP5306 button's position
(`KEY_IN`) against yours.

The website shows the parts in 3D (`site/pages/wiki/models.html`) and covers printing, wiring and assembly
(`site/pages/wiki/print.html`).
